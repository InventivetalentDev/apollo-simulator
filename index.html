Apollo Guidance Computer

Apollo 11 flight software, assembled from the 1969 source and running in your browser

Try it

Click a command to key it into the DSKY, or type on your keyboard: digits, Verb, Noun, Enter, Clear, Pro, Key rel, Reset.

    What you are looking at

    • The DSKY was the crew's keyboard and display. Every lamp and digit here is set by the original 1969 software, through the same I/O channels the real hardware used.
    • The listing is the actual Apollo 11 source. Your browser assembles it into the 36 banks of core rope, and an emulated Block II computer runs it at 85,333 memory cycles a second.
    • The source comes from chrislgarry/Apollo-11 on GitHub. Click any line number in the listing, or the file and line under “Running”, to open the same line there.
    • In the LM, the computer is wired to a simulated spacecraft: the IMU and its angle counters, the 16 RCS jets, the hand controller, the descent engine and the LM's orbit around the Moon. The autopilot and the descent guidance are the original code. The landing radar answers but has no data.
